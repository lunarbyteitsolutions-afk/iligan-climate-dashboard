Rainfall

How much rain has fallen, and for how long a barangay has gone without it, is one of the clearest early signals of El Niño drought stress — and one of the clearest signals that the dry spell has broken. Tracking it per barangay lets the city see which areas are driest before water shortages are reported, not after.

Owning Office

PAGASA is the authoritative source for rainfall in the Philippines. CDRRMO (City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office) is the likely holder of any local rain-gauge readings supplementing PAGASA's station data. Confirmation of which office holds barangay-level readings is itself part of this request.
