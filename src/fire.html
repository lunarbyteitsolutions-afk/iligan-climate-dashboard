Fire Incidents

Dry vegetation and extended heat raise fire risk — grass fires, agricultural burning that gets away, and structural fires alike. Confirmed incident counts per barangay tell the city where dry-season fire response burden is concentrated, distinct from a satellite guess at where something is burning.

Owning Office

BFP Iligan City (Bureau of Fire Protection) for structural and grass fire incidents. ICENRO (City Environment and Natural Resources Office) for vegetation and watershed fire incidents.

A related but different signal

Satellite-detected thermal anomalies (from NASA FIRMS) are planned as a separate, clearly-labeled supplementary layer once built — they are not confirmed fire incidents, and will not fill in this indicator. A satellite hotspot can be a grass fire, agricultural burning, or a false positive; a structural fire can produce no hotspot at all. This page's "Fire Incidents" indicator stays NO DATA / PENDING until BFP Iligan City or ICENRO provide confirmed records, regardless of what the satellite layer shows.
