Population Exposed

Heat index tells you where it's hot. Population exposed tells you how many people that heat actually reaches — the first indicator that crosses Environmental conditions with Social reality. It's the difference between "Extreme Caution in 43 barangays" and "roughly how many Iliganons are living under Extreme Caution right now."

Owning Office

CSWDO (City Social Welfare and Development Office) is named as the owning office for this indicator. This page is a request to confirm that assignment and the population methodology below with CSWDO before treating any resulting figure as more than a derived estimate.

What This Will (and Won't) Be

Once built, this indicator will cross each barangay's PSA population baseline against its current heat band, and report a population-by-band split (e.g. "X of Y Iliganons are in Extreme Caution heat conditions right now"). Three caveats will be permanently visible wherever this number appears:

FieldTypeUnitExample

This will never be phrased as "affected," "at risk of heat stroke," or any health outcome — only as an exposure estimate: people living in a barangay currently reporting that band. It will not become a Social Vulnerability Score — that needs vulnerable-group counts from CSWDO, OSCA, PDAO and City Health, and inventing weights before those inputs exist is exactly the derived-number overreach this project's rules forbid.
