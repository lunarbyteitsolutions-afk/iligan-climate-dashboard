READ BEFORE CITING THIS NUMBER
  1. Population baseline is disputed, pending PSA verification. The 2024 figures used here (city total 368,132; Tubod 31,813) don't reconcile with 2020 census figures found during the same lookup (city total 363,115; Tubod 33,243) — Tubod, the city's largest barangay, would have to have fallen ~4.6% while the city total grew ~1.4%, which isn't ordinary growth. Treat every population figure on this page as reported, not verified, until confirmed directly against psa.gov.ph.
  2. This assumes barangay-wide exposure at the reference point's heat index. Actual exposure varies with elevation, shade, housing, and whether someone works outdoors — a barangay's population isn't uniformly experiencing one single number.
  3. This is an exposure estimate, not a count of people harmed. It never means "affected," "at risk of heat stroke," or any health outcome — only that a barangay's population is currently reporting that heat band. "In these conditions" is the honest phrasing; anything stronger is not supported by this data.

Methodology & Owning Office

CSWDO (City Social Welfare and Development Office) is named as the owning office for this indicator. This page's methodology — crossing each barangay's PSA population baseline against its current heat band — has not yet been confirmed with CSWDO; treat the split above as a derived estimate pending that confirmation, on top of the population-baseline dispute above.

This indicator will not become a Social Vulnerability Score. That needs vulnerable-group counts from CSWDO, OSCA, PDAO and City Health, and inventing weights before those inputs exist is exactly the derived-number overreach this project's rules forbid.
